TEAM CHARTER
Team G29 The Northstar Sprint

This is a group guide and instructions that can be followed during the entire period and it  isn't about being strict, it's about making sure everyone feels and gets involved, supported, and heard, so we can deliver our best work together.
1. Communication
We'll use WhatsApp as our main channel for day-to-day updates and questions. If you're stuck, busy, or need help, just say so no one’s expected to figure everything out alone. Expected response time: within 6 hours during the day. If you know you'll be offline longer than that, give the team a heads-up in advance.
2. Deadlines & Check-ins
We'll do a quick daily check-in at 8.00 p.m, just a short update on what you did, what you're doing next, and anything blocking you. If you're going to miss a task deadline, tell the team before it's due, not after, so we can adjust together.
3. Everyone Contributes
We all have different strengths, so let's make sure tasks are spread out fairly and everyone gets a meaningful piece of the work.This will also keep our contribution log balanced, which is part of what's graded.
4. Own Our Tasks
Once you take a task, you own moving it forward and keeping the board updated on it. If you're stuck, flag it early rather than sitting on it quietly.
5. Keep the Board Updated The Same Day
Move your task's status the same day you work on it, not in a batch at the end of the week. This is what makes our audit trail honest and trustworthy.
6. Help Each Other
If someone's struggling or falling behind, we support them rather than leaving them to figure it out alone,that's what teammates are for.
7. Conflict Resolution
If two of us disagree on an approach, we'll talk it out directly first. If we can't agree within a reasonable time, we bring it to the full team to vote or decide together, and we move forward as one team once a decision is made with no lingering side-tension.
8. Escalation Path
If a teammate shows zero activity or goes silent for 2+ days, here's what we do:
•     Day 1 of silence: a teammate reaches out directly to check in.
•     Day 2 of silence: if still no response, we flag it in the group chat and reassign their task if the deadline is at risk.
•     If it continues, we raise it with the instructor rather than let it affect the whole team's grade.
9. Quality Over Rushing
Let's review and test each other's work before it's final, so what we submit is something we're all proud of.
10. No Silly Questions
Ask early rather than struggle silently. That's better for you and for the team's timeline.
11. Let's Enjoy the Process 
Five days, five people, different skills  let's use everyone's strengths and have each other's backs.
